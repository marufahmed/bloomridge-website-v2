# Meta ad copy

Images are in `public/infographics/`. Ad wording never says or implies that the reader's own
child has a difficulty (Meta rejects that), so it talks about "children" and "parents" in general.
No assessment price is quoted, because the site does not publish one.

## Campaign 1: Leads to WhatsApp (about 70% of budget)

Objective: Leads. Conversion location: WhatsApp. Button: Send WhatsApp message.
Audience: people living within 5 km of the centre, aged 25 to 45, parents of toddlers,
preschoolers and early school-age children.
One ad set, three ads: Carousel A, Single 1, Single 2.

### Carousel A: "What am I seeing?"

Primary text:
> Late talking, big reactions to noise, two languages at home. Parents in Banasree ask us about these every week. Five short guides from our team, and a WhatsApp number if you would like to talk it through.

## Campaign 3: Reach (about 30% of budget)

Objective: Awareness. Same 5 km area. Use "existing post" and pick the two or three organic
posts with the most shares and saves. No new copy needed; the post caption is the ad.
Do not pick a post whose caption opens with a question about "your child".

## WhatsApp setup

Greeting message:
> Thank you for messaging Bloomridge Springs. Tell us your child's age and what you would like help with, and one of our team will reply shortly.

Suggested questions shown to the parent (set these in the ad's message template):
1. How does a first assessment work?
2. What do the monthly plans include?
3. Where are you in Banasree, and when can I visit?

Away message, outside working hours:
> Thank you for your message. We are closed just now and will reply when we open. If you would like a call back, please send your name and a good time to ring.
